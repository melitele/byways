The 46-mile Cottonwood Canyons Scenic Backway runs through Grand Staircase-Escalante National Monument between US-89 near Paria Ranger Station and Cannonville on Scenic Byway 12. The southern entrance is about 64 miles east of Kanab on US-89, while the northern entrance is in Cannonville, following signs for Kodachrome Basin State Park. The road is paved for the 9 miles between Cannonville and the park turnoff, and unpaved graded gravel over clay for the rest of the distance. The road, suitable for 2WD passenger cars in good weather but potentially impassable to 4x4 vehicles when wet, can be traversed in either direction.
